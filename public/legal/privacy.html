Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

Information we use

Wyte uses your GitHub account identity, repository access, requested repository content, service preferences, notification tokens, diagnostic usage counts, and billing records needed to operate the service.

Repository content

Repository data is requested from GitHub only when needed for actions you initiate. We do not sell private repository content.

AI diagnostics

Code and diagnostic context may be sent to configured AI providers to provide the requested diagnosis. Wyte redacts common credential patterns before sending diagnostic context and does not ask AI providers to commit or push code.

Payments

Payment information is handled through Flutterwave's payment APIs. Wyte does not intentionally store full card numbers, CVV values, PINs, or OTPs.

Notifications

If you enable push notifications, Wyte stores the push token, account identifier, and device timezone needed to deliver build alerts, Good Morning messages, repository-limit reminders, and renewal reminders. You can disable notifications in Settings or your browser.

Security and retention

Authentication sessions, billing records, and notification registrations are protected server-side. Data is retained only as needed for service operation, security, accounting, and legal obligations.
