Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

Information we use

WyteLab uses your GitHub account identity and repository access, plus requested repository content, service preferences, notification tokens, diagnostic usage counts, and billing records needed to operate the service. Optional Google Drive integration is separate and is used only when you choose to connect it.

Repository content

Repository data is requested from GitHub only when needed for actions you initiate. We do not sell private repository content.

AI diagnostics

Code and diagnostic context may be sent to configured AI providers to provide the requested diagnosis. WyteLab redacts common credential patterns before sending diagnostic context and does not ask AI providers to commit or push code.

Payments

Payment information is handled through Flutterwave's payment APIs. WyteLab does not intentionally store full card numbers, CVV values, PINs, or OTPs.

Notifications

If you enable push notifications, WyteLab stores the push token, account identifier, and device timezone needed to deliver build alerts, Good Morning messages, repository-limit reminders, and renewal reminders. You can disable notifications in Settings or your browser.

Security and retention

Authentication sessions, billing records, and notification registrations are protected server-side. Data is retained only as needed for service operation, security, accounting, and legal obligations.

Google Drive integration

If you choose to connect Google Drive, WyteLab requests the drive.file scope only — this lets WyteLab create and access only the specific files it creates in your Drive (repository ZIP snapshots you export), and nothing else in your Drive. WyteLab does not request access to your existing Drive files, Gmail, Calendar, Docs, or Sheets.

Access tokens are encrypted at rest and used only to upload the ZIP snapshot you requested at that moment. WyteLab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: this data is never used for advertising, never sold, and is not used to train generalized AI/ML models.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from within WyteLab (Developer Hub → Disconnect Drive), which revokes the stored authorization and deletes it from our records, or by removing WyteLab's access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
